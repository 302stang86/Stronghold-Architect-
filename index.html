Raise your stronghold

New keeps start with exactly one throne, one castle entrance, 1–2 hallways, and 1–2 empty rooms. The entrance starts in a random column on the bottom row, with the connected keep extending upward. The immediate left, right, and below the entrance cannot be built on. Existing saved layouts are preserved. Tap a pulsing green + beside your keep to choose an unlocked room or hallway. The black field supports 18×18 tiles. Rotate previews in the build popup for free to match doorways before building. Rotating a placed room or hallway costs 10 gold per successful change; invalid positions are skipped without charge.

Hallways, Empty Rooms, Gold Mines, and Research Rooms are available immediately. Build a Research Room, wait for a game day to pass to earn 10 research points, then choose a blueprint in the research panel or build menu to unlock it permanently. All other rooms require research. Libraries add 5 extra research points daily after being researched.

Gold Mines add 25 gold daily. The throne produces 20 gold and 12 stone per day, accumulated as one-minute room batches that require collection. Each tile consumes 1 food daily; kitchens produce 12. Food shortages cost 8 integrity. Raids strike every third day. Each day lasts 10 real minutes while the game is open. Income and raids resolve automatically at day boundaries. Raids occur every third day, and the campaign continues indefinitely until the keep falls. Time pauses when the game is closed.

There is exactly one throne and one entrance; neither can be demolished. Select the throne and choose Relocate for 50 gold and 25 stone, then select a connected open square or Empty Room. The former throne tile becomes an Empty Room, preserving the keep's paths.

Select a tile to see its name and details. Demolition refunds half the construction cost, provided every remaining tile stays connected. Repair restores 25 integrity for 30 gold. Saves from the earlier version are upgraded automatically; existing rooms stay researched.

Room tiles are enlarged for future MC and NPC characters; use + and − to zoom. Select an Empty Room to install one trap style: Spiked (50 gold), Gas (100), Flame (175), or Acid Rain (275). Their enemy-raid defense increases in that order. Traps never target allies. Demolition costs 25% of the trap price, rounded up, and must be paid before replacing a trap, demolishing its room, or moving the throne into it.

Mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer inside the field; two-finger pinch zooms on touchscreens. Drag with one finger to pan. Double-click or double-tap an occupied room for an enlarged view, details, and existing upgrades. Assign collectors in a producing room’s details. Collectors physically walk to the room, pick up its batch, carry it to the shared Vault (or temporary throne storage), and return. Their deliveries receive exactly +3%. You start with one free collector; extra hires start at 75 gold and double after each purchase. Uncollected room batches never stack. Manual icon clicks deposit instantly without the bonus. Theft and room combat remain planned features.

The MC uses your evil-prince character design. He wanders only on the throne room’s central floor, pauses between steps, scales with zoom, and remains assigned to that room after relocation. Double-click the throne room for a larger view of him.
