Raise your stronghold

Your keep starts with one throne, one castle gate on the bottom edge, one or two hallways and one or two empty rooms on a 24 by 24 field. Tap a pulsing green + beside your keep to build. Rotate the previews in the build menu to match doorways. The stone wall marks the tiles beside the gate that cannot be built on.

Hallways, Empty Rooms, Gold Mines and Research Rooms are available at once. Research Rooms make research points that permanently unlock other rooms, and some rooms also need another room built first. Rotating a placed room costs 10 gold. Demolishing a room costs 50 gold with no refund. Relocating the throne costs 50 gold and 25 stone.

Producing rooms fill a one-minute batch that does not stack. Tap the icon on a room to collect it, or assign a servant to the room: servants collect automatically and earn a 5% bonus. The prince collects the throne room’s gold and stone himself.

Tap a servant on the map, or open the servants list on the right, to see details and assign a room. Servants without a room wander the keep. Assign a servant by tapping a room’s servant list as well. You can hold three servants; each Kitchen allows more (Level 1 adds one, Level 3 two in total, Level 5 four in total).

Food: the prince and every servant eat 1 food per day. After 1 day without food servants fight 30% weaker. After 2 days the prince fights 25% weaker. From day 3 one servant starves each day. On day 5 the prince loses 20% of his battle health, and on day 7 he dies and the keep falls. One fed day removes all penalties.

Invaders arrive from day 3, every two to four days. They enter at the gate, visit rooms that hold servants, and finish at the throne room. Unassigned servants hide in the throne room. Traps hurt invaders but never take them below 10% health. A defeated invader drops loot. If the prince loses, 50% of your gold, 30% of your stone, 30% of your food and 10% of your research points are stolen.

Tap a room to select it and double-tap for a closer view. Use + and − or the mouse wheel to zoom. Time pauses when the game is closed or you leave the tab.
